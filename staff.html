Messages expire after two minutes or when the dive ends. Between-dive help requests expire after 15 minutes. Displayed counts show tablets that showed the message, not whether someone read it. Sleeping tablets and external apps may miss it.

No recent check-in can mean a tablet is asleep, using another app, or disconnected. Status is reported by the tablet homepage, not a device-management service.

Check-ins contain no patient names. The list resets when the server restarts. Tablets appear after their updated homepage is opened.
