Chamber announcement

Delivery and expiry

Announcements last two minutes or until the dive ends. Between-dive help requests last 15 minutes. Displayed counts confirm a tablet showed the message, not that someone read it. Sleeping tablets and external apps may miss it.

Guest chat controls

Guests message another seat in their current dive. Between dives, empty-seat test chat is available. Pausing clears the chamber’s conversations and stays in effect until you resume it.

Tablets & updates

Open a suite to check individual tablets. Refresh an older tablet’s homepage when the guest is finished.

Tap a suite to see its tablets. “Recently connected” means the homepage contacted us within 2½ minutes. “No recent contact” may mean sleep, another app, or a lost connection—not necessarily a broken tablet.

    How to update a tablet

    Reload the tablet homepage when the guest is finished, then reopen Games Hub. Check its Settings gear for the version. Tablets & updates shows the last version each tablet sent; sleeping tablets may not report.

    No recent check-in may mean a tablet is asleep, using another app, or disconnected. Open its homepage to reconnect. Guest names on help requests come from the current seat assignment; names are not saved in tablet check-ins or the request queue.
